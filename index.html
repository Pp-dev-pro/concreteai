02 How to Use

1

Enter the mix

Use kg per m³ of concrete from the batch ticket. Enter 0 for slag, fly ash or superplasticizer if absent.

2

Set the age

Age in days since casting (1 to 365). The same mix gives very different results at 7 and 28 days.

3

Predict

The values are sent securely to the Catalyst QuickML endpoint, which runs the trained model.

4

Read with care

Compare with the target mean strength. Keep a margin of about 5 MPa for model error.

03 Model & Dataset

The engine is a Deep Forest (Random Forest + ExtraTrees layers) trained on the UCI Concrete Compressive Strength dataset (ID 165, I-Cheng Yeh): 822 training and 208 test samples, no missing values.
